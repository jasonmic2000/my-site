Background options v2: dots vs grid

How to judge: each column shows light mode (top) and dark mode (bottom) at the same time, on a 24px unit that matches your body text line height. Look at the faintest marks you can see around the text on your monitor, then drag Strength. Under each panel is the worst-case background pixel behind text and whether body text, muted text and your accent still pass WCAG (4.5:1 text, 3:1 large accent / focus ring). Columns A and B use the slightly deeper light accent #e5456a (so dark-ink marks are safe); column C keeps today's #eb506d and uses white highlight lines instead. Dark mode is identical in all three. Text here is not snapped to the grid yet (that comes with the final design polish).
