Font pairings Look at the body text, the dates and the name. Open this on your own monitor.
1 - Today (baseline)

Geist for UI and headings, your operating system's serif (Georgia on Windows) for reading text.

2 - One family

Geist everywhere, Geist Mono for dates and meta. Cohesive and technical; least personality, least risk.

3 - Geist + Newsreader (recommended)

Sans for UI, work and headings; a warm bundled serif (Newsreader) reserved for the bio and blog posts. Mono for meta.

4 - Geist + Source Serif 4

Same idea, sturdier serif designed for screens. Slightly more neutral than Newsreader.

5 - Atkinson Hyperlegible + JetBrains Mono

Built for legibility (fits your accessibility principle): distinct letterforms, sturdy strokes. Friendlier and a bit quirky.

6 - Bricolage Grotesque headings

A characterful grotesque for the name and post titles only; Geist for everything you read.

7 - Fraunces headings

A soft, slightly wonky serif for the name and post titles; the most playful, heaviest personality.
