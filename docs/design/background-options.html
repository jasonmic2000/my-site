Background options

How to judge: open this on your own monitor and look at the faintest line you can see in the body text area. Drag Strength up to see more; the table under each option shows the worst-case background pixel behind text and whether body text, muted text and your accent colour still pass WCAG (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for the large accent heading and focus ring). Untick the highlight box to see ordinary dark lines in light mode, where the accent colour runs out of headroom very quickly.
